About PixOrbital

Data science and AI, built to hold up in production.

PixOrbital designs, builds, and deploys machine learning and AI systems for organizations that need more than a proof of concept. We work across the full lifecycle — from messy source data to models that run reliably in production — with an approach grounded in statistical rigor and software engineering discipline.

Our mission is straightforward: turn data into decisions. Every project starts with the business question, not the algorithm, and ends with something a team can actually run, monitor, and trust.

Approach

Outcome

Research Project

Predictive Energy Intelligence

Forecasting energy consumption using machine learning and time-series methods.

Problem
Facilities teams often plan energy usage reactively, without visibility into near-term demand.
Approach
Time-series forecasting models trained on historical consumption and weather data.
Technology

Result

Internal Case Study

Intelligent Document Assistant

Document ingestion, chunking, embeddings, retrieval, and LLM responses.

Problem
Teams lose hours searching scattered PDFs and internal docs for specific answers.
Approach
Document ingestion → chunking → embeddings → retrieval → LLM responses grounded in source text.
Technology
Python, vector database, retrieval-augmented generation, LLM API
Result
A working assistant that answers questions directly from an internal document set, with source citations.

Technology

Result
